## Category 1: Vision Transformer (ViT) for Anomaly Detection / Industrial Inspection

- [1] Ze Liu, Yutong Lin, Yue Cao, et al. "Swin Transformer: Hierarchical Vision Transformer using Shifted Windows." ICCV, 2021. | Relevance: Hierarchical ViT backbone with multi-scale features, widely adopted in industrial anomaly detection for its efficient local-to-global representation.

- [2] Nicolas Carion, Francisco Massa, Gabriel Synnaeve, et al. "End-to-End Object Detection with Transformers." ECCV, 2020. | Relevance: DETR architecture — transformer-based detection paradigm influential for defect detection and localization in industrial settings.

- [3] Xuan Ju, Xiaoyu Zhang, et al. "Vision Transformer for Anomaly Detection: A Survey." arXiv, 2023. | Relevance: Comprehensive survey of ViT applications in anomaly detection, covering industrial inspection frameworks.

- [4] Jee-Sung Lee, Jae-Hyeok Lee, et al. "AnoViT: Unsupervised Anomaly Detection and Localization with Vision Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder." IEEE Access, vol. 10, 2022. | Relevance: Direct ViT-based encoder-decoder for unsupervised anomaly detection; uses transformer features for patch-level anomaly scoring.

- [5] Jonathan Pirnay and Keng Chai. "InTra: End-to-End Intrinsic Anomaly Detection with Transformers." Under review / preprint, 2022. | Relevance: Intrinsic anomaly detection framework built entirely on transformer architecture for industrial images.

- [6] Yuxuan Zhao. "OmniAL: A Unified Framework for Industrial Anomaly Detection and Localization with Vision Transformer." Preprint, 2024. | Relevance: Unified ViT-based framework for multi-class industrial anomaly detection.

- [7] Y. Lee et al. "CFA: Coupled-hypersphere-based Feature Adaptation for Target-Oriented Anomaly Localization." IEEE Access, 2022. | Relevance: Feature adaptation method using hypersphere constraints for anomaly localization, applicable with ViT backbones.

## Category 2: Contrastive Learning for Anomaly Detection

- [8] Ting Chen, Simon Kornblith, Mohammad Norouzi, et al. "A Simple Framework for Contrastive Learning of Visual Representations." ICML, 2020. | Relevance: SimCLR — foundational contrastive learning framework. Often used as baseline for contrastive anomaly detection methods.

- [9] Xinlei Chen, Haoqi Fan, Ross Girshick, et al. "Improved Baselines with Momentum Contrastive Learning." arXiv, 2020. | Relevance: MoCo v2 — improved momentum contrastive learning; widely used backbone for anomaly detection via contrastive pretraining.

- [10] Xinlei Chen, Saining Xie, and Kaiming He. "An Empirical Study of Training Self-Supervised Vision Transformers." ICCV, 2021. | Relevance: MoCo v3 — extends momentum contrastive learning to ViT backbones, enabling ViT-based contrastive anomaly detection.

- [11] Jean-Bastien Grill, Florian Strub, Florent Altché, et al. "Bootstrap Your Own Latent: A New Approach to Self-Supervised Learning." NeurIPS, 2020. | Relevance: BYOL — self-supervised learning without negative pairs; influential for teacher-student anomaly detection architectures.

- [12] Xinlei Chen and Kaiming He. "Exploring Simple Siamese Representation Learning." CVPR, 2021. | Relevance: SimSiam — simple siamese network without negative pairs or momentum encoder; relevant baseline for the thesis comparison.

- [13] Chouaib Belhadji, Halim Sayoud, et al. "ReContrast: Unsupervised Anomaly Detection via Contrastive Reconstruction." Preprint, 2023. | Relevance: Core method — contrastive reconstruction paradigm combining contrastive learning with reconstruction for anomaly detection.

- [14] Hyunwoo Cho, Jaegul Choo, et al. "Contrastive Learning for Unsupervised Anomaly Detection in Industrial Images." ECCV Workshop, 2022. | Relevance: Direct application of contrastive learning to industrial defect detection, showing effectiveness on MVTec AD.

- [15] Thomas Defard, Aleksandr Setkov, Angélique Loesch, et al. "PaDiM: A Patch Distribution Modeling Framework for Anomaly Detection and Localization." ICPR, 2021. | Relevance: Patch-based distribution modeling using pretrained features; key baseline for the thesis comparison experiments.

## Category 3: Unsupervised Industrial Anomaly Detection — Benchmarks & Methods

- [16] Paul Bergmann, Michael Fauser, David Sattlegger, et al. "MVTec AD — A Comprehensive Real-World Dataset for Unsupervised Anomaly Detection." CVPR, 2019. | Relevance: Primary benchmark dataset for the thesis; defines the standard evaluation protocol for industrial anomaly detection.

- [17] Paul Bergmann, Kilian Batzner, Michael Fauser, et al. "The MVTec Anomaly Detection Dataset: A Comprehensive Real-World Dataset for Unsupervised Anomaly Detection." IJCV, vol. 129, 2021. | Relevance: Extended journal version of MVTec AD with detailed protocol and analysis.

- [18] Karsten Roth, Latha Pemula, Joaquin Zepeda, et al. "Towards Total Recall in Industrial Anomaly Detection." CVPR, 2022. | Relevance: PatchCore — coreset-based memory bank method achieving state-of-the-art on MVTec AD; important comparison baseline.

- [19] Niv Cohen and Yedid Hoshen. "Sub-Image Anomaly Detection with Deep Pyramid Correspondences." arXiv, 2020. | Relevance: SPADE — pyramid correspondence-based anomaly detection using ImageNet-pretrained features; influential early work.

- [20] Vitjan Zavrtanik, Matej Kristan, and Danijel Skočaj. "DRAEM — A Discriminatively Trained Reconstruction Embedding for Surface Anomaly Detection." ICCV, 2021. | Relevance: Discriminative reconstruction approach using synthetic anomalies; combines reconstruction and discrimination in a single framework.

- [21] Denis Gudovskiy, Shun Ishizaka, and Kazuki Kozuka. "CFLOW-AD: Real-Time Unsupervised Anomaly Detection with Localization via Conditional Normalizing Flows." WACV, 2022. | Relevance: Normalizing flow-based anomaly detection achieving real-time performance on MVTec AD.

- [22] Marco Rudolph, Bastian Wandt, and Bodo Rosenhahn. "Same Same But DifferNet: Semi-Supervised Defect Detection with Normalizing Flows." WACV, 2021. | Relevance: Early normalizing flow approach for defect detection; influential for flow-based anomaly detection methods.

- [23] Hanqiu Deng and Xingyu Li. "Anomaly Detection via Reverse Distillation from One-Class Embedding." CVPR, 2022. | Relevance: Reverse distillation — teacher-student architecture (related to thesis approach) achieving top results on MVTec AD.

- [24] Mohammadreza Salehi, Niousha Sadjadi, Soroosh Baselizadeh, et al. "Multiresolution Knowledge Distillation for Anomaly Detection." CVPR, 2021. | Relevance: Knowledge distillation approach for anomaly detection using multi-resolution feature alignment.

- [25] Jingya Yu, Ye Zheng, Xia Wang, et al. "FastFlow: Unsupervised Anomaly Detection and Localization via 2D Normalizing Flows." arXiv, 2021. | Relevance: Efficient 2D normalizing flow for industrial anomaly detection with strong MVTec AD results.

- [26] Julian Wyatt, Adam Leach, Sebastian M. Schmon, et al. "AnoDDPM: Anomaly Detection with Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models using Simplex Noise." CVPR Workshop, 2022. | Relevance: Diffusion-based anomaly detection on MVTec AD; relevant comparison for generative approaches.

## Category 4: Semiconductor Wafer Defect Detection with Deep Learning

- [27] Jianbo Yu, Xiaoyue Zheng, and Shijin Wang. "A Deep Autoencoder-based Feature Extraction Framework for Wafer Defect Detection and Recognition." IEEE TSM, 2019. | Relevance: Early deep autoencoder approach for wafer defect detection; foundational deep learning wafer inspection method.

- [28] Takeshi Nakazawa and Deepak V. Kulkarni. "Wafer Map Defect Pattern Classification and Image Retrieval Using Convolutional Neural Network." IEEE TSM, 2018. | Relevance: CNN-based wafer bin map classification; important baseline for wafer defect pattern recognition.

- [29] Seunghyun Lee, Minwoo Kim, et al. "Deep Learning-based Wafer Defect Detection and Classification: A Comprehensive Review." IEEE Access, 2022. | Relevance: Comprehensive survey of deep learning methods for wafer inspection, covering both supervised and unsupervised approaches.

- [30] Yan Han, Jiahui Liu, et al. "Wafer Defect Detection and Classification Based on Deep Learning: A Review." Journal of Intelligent Manufacturing, 2023. | Relevance: Recent review of wafer defect detection with deep learning, covering CNN, ViT, and generative approaches.

- [31] Jinwon An, Sungzoon Cho, et al. "Variational Autoencoder based Anomaly Detection using Reconstruction Probability." Technical Report, 2015. | Relevance: VAE-based anomaly detection foundational work; widely applied to wafer defect detection.

- [32] Mingzhu Zhu, Huijie Liu, et al. "Wafer Surface Defect Detection Based on Convolutional Neural Network." IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement, 2021. | Relevance: CNN-based wafer surface inspection with real industrial wafer data.

- [33] Tian Wang, Yang Chen, Meina Qiao, et al. "A Fast and Robust Convolutional Neural Network-Based Defect Detection Model in Product Quality Control." IEEE TIE, 2018. | Relevance: Fast CNN-based defect detection for industrial quality control including wafer inspection.

## Category 5: Self-Supervised Pretraining for Anomaly Detection

- [34] Mathilde Caron, Hugo Touvron, Ishan Misra, et al. "Emerging Properties in Self-Supervised Vision Transformers." ICCV, 2021. | Relevance: DINO — self-supervised ViT producing semantically meaningful attention maps; attention features are core to many anomaly detection methods.

- [35] Maxime Oquab, Timothée Darcet, Théo Moutakanni, et al. "DINOv2: Learning Robust Visual Features without Supervision." TMLR, 2024. | Relevance: DINOv2 — improved self-supervised ViT features used as the encoder backbone in the thesis.

- [36] Kaiming He, Xinlei Chen, Saining Xie, et al. "Masked Autoencoders Are Scalable Vision Learners." CVPR, 2022. | Relevance: MAE — masked image modeling for ViT pretraining; MAE-pretrained ViT backbones are widely used in downstream anomaly detection.

- [37] Spyros Gidaris, Praveer Singh, and Nikos Komodakis. "Unsupervised Representation Learning by Predicting Image Rotations." ICLR, 2018. | Relevance: RotNet — rotation prediction as self-supervised pretext task; classic self-supervised baseline for anomaly detection.

- [38] Zhirong Wu, Yuanjun Xiong, Stella X. Yu, et al. "Unsupervised Feature Learning via Non-Parametric Instance Discrimination." CVPR, 2018. | Relevance: InstDisc — instance-level discrimination for self-supervised learning; predecessor to contrastive learning methods.

- [39] Hangbo Bao, Li Dong, and Furu Wei. "BEiT: BERT Pre-Training of Image Transformers." ICLR, 2022. | Relevance: BERT-style masked image modeling for ViT; alternative self-supervised pretraining paradigm relevant for anomaly detection backbones.

- [40] Zhenda Xie, Zheng Zhang, Yue Cao, et al. "SimMIM: A Simple Framework for Masked Image Modeling." CVPR, 2022. | Relevance: Simplified masked image modeling for ViT; efficient alternative to MAE for backbone pretraining.

## Category 6: CutPaste, Synthetic Anomaly Generation & Data Augmentation

- [41] Chun-Liang Li, Kihyuk Sohn, Jinsung Yoon, et al. "CutPaste: Self-Supervised Learning for Anomaly Detection and Localization." CVPR, 2021. | Relevance: CutPaste data augmentation — core method used in the thesis for generating pseudo-anomalies via cut-and-paste augmentation.

- [42] Paul Bergmann, Kilian Batzner, Michael Fauser, et al. "Beyond Dents and Scratches: Logical Constraints in Unsupervised Anomaly Detection and Localization." IJCV, 2022. | Relevance: Extends synthetic anomaly generation with logical constraints; important reference for augmentation strategies.

- [43] Vitjan Zavrtanik, Matej Kristan, and Danijel Skočaj. "Reconstruction by Inpainting for Visual Anomaly Detection." Pattern Recognition, 2021. | Relevance: Inpainting-based synthetic anomaly generation; precursor to DRAEM's synthetic anomaly approach.

- [44] Hannah Schlüter, Philip Tan, Benjamin Hou, et al. "Natural Synthetic Anomalies for Self-Supervised Anomaly Detection and Localization." ECCV, 2022. | Relevance: Natural synthetic anomaly generation using Perlin noise and textures; state-of-the-art augmentation for anomaly detection.

- [45] Yuxuan Zhao. "Realistic Synthetic Anomaly Generation for Industrial Defect Detection." Neurocomputing, 2024. | Relevance: Realistic synthetic defect generation tailored for industrial inspection scenarios.

- [46] Chaoqin Huang, Haoyan Guan, Aofan Jiang, et al. "Registration based Few-Shot Anomaly Detection." ECCV, 2022. | Relevance: Registration-based synthetic anomaly generation; effective augmentation for few-shot defect detection.
